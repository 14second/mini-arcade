Goose Grab

Two scarecrows, one goose, zero dignity. Hop to her, grab her, and throw her into the other pen. First to 5 — or best score when the 2:00 clock runs out.

Careful: hold her past 3 seconds and she wriggles free, get tackled and you drop her — and sometimes she flaps mid-throw… right back toward your pen. Own goals count. For them.

Defense: hop into her flight path to snatch her mid-air!

Touch: tap / drag your half to hop · while carrying, a quick tap throws her at their pen — tap left or right to pick the spot.
Keys: WASD + SPACE (blue) · Arrows + ENTER (red).

🏆

Blue wins!
